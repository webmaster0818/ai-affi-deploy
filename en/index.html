What is it

What is AI video generation?

AI video generation is the practice of using AI to assemble narration, captions and footage from a script, image or existing video. Workflows that used to consume hours of shooting, editing and recording shrink to minutes.

Roughly, the field splits into four families: (1) text-to-video tools that build voiced videos from articles or scripts (Fliki, Pictory), (2) avatar tools where an AI presenter reads your script (Synthesia, HeyGen), (3) browser-based online editors that handle captions and editing (Veed) and (4) generative video tools that synthesize the footage itself (Runway).

The right type depends on your goal. We organize each tool using only its public information so you can pick without second-guessing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI video generation tool?＋

It's a tool that takes a script, image or existing footage and uses AI to assemble narration, captions and visuals into a finished video. You don't need filming or advanced editing skills to ship video at speed.

Where should a beginner start?＋

If you want to turn text or articles into voiced video, Fliki is the easiest entry point — it's simple to operate and offers a free plan. For training videos with talking avatars, look at Synthesia or HeyGen; for generating footage from scratch, Runway is the natural pick.

Are there free options?＋

Most of the tools we cover offer a free plan or a free trial. Free plans typically include a watermark and limits on export time or credits, so use them to validate quality before paying.

Is the pricing on this site accurate?＋

All pricing and program terms reflect each tool's public information as of June 2026. Pricing changes without notice, so always confirm the latest on the official site before signing up.
