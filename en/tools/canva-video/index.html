What is Canva Video AI? Video AI inside a design platform

Canva Video AI is the umbrella name for Canva's Magic Studio video AI features — including text-to-video via Magic Design, video translation, AI narration and image-to-video. Capabilities ship continuously.

Unlike single-purpose video AI tools, the unique advantage is that Canva's gigantic template, asset, font and brand kit library carries straight over to video. Banners, social images, presentations, print and video all live in the same workflow — for teams already using Canva, the learning curve is essentially zero.

Key features

1. Magic Design for Video

Prompts and keywords return ready-to-edit video templates with Canva's assets, text and transitions already laid in. Fine-tune the content and you've got a polished social or promo video. Especially strong for rapid drafts.

2. AI narration and auto-captions

Generate narration audio from text; auto-generate captions from video. Both work in Japanese, making it a strong fit for Japanese-market social production.

3. Video translation (Translate)

Video translation features that swap captions and audio across languages keep expanding. 100+ languages supported. Lip-sync polish trails HeyGen, but the all-in-Canva convenience is a real advantage.

4. Brand kit and team collaboration

Manage logos, colors, fonts and templates as a brand kit and share them across the team — multiple producers stay on-brand. Built for comms and marketing team production at scale.

Pricing (as of June 2026)

Canva Video AI ships across Canva Free / Canva Pro (around $13/mo) / Canva Teams.

  • Free: basic video editing and some AI features with usage caps.
  • Canva Pro (~$13/mo): Magic Studio AI, premium assets, brand kit, high-res export. The default for individuals and small businesses.
  • Canva Teams: multi-seat brand governance, admin and approval flows.

Compared to other AI video tools running $20–$60/mo, Canva Pro at around $13/mo covers design and video AI together — strong on value. Pricing and plan composition change — always confirm with the official site before signing up.

Who Canva Video AI is for

  • Anyone already on Canva who wants to add video AI without a new tool
  • Solo creators and brands producing vertical short-form for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts
  • Teams running images, banners and video on one brand-governed workflow
  • Producers who value strong Japanese fonts and Japanese UI

For long-form narrated YouTube explainers use Fliki or InVideo AI; for AI avatars use HeyGen; for cinematic generative footage use Sora or Runway.

The verdict

Canva Video AI plays a different game from purpose-built AI video tools — it's the design platform that absorbed video AI. The value story (around $13/mo for design plus video plus social workflow), strong Japanese coverage and brand governance make it especially strong for social-first production. Pair it with another tool for long-form narration, and let Canva own everything Canva is best at.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Canva Video AI for free?＋

Canva itself has a free plan, and you can sample basic video editing and some AI features without paying. Full Magic Studio AI usage (Magic Design, video translation, AI narration etc.) assumes Canva Pro (around $13/mo) and above; the free plan caps generation count and feature access.

Does Canva work well in Japanese?＋

Canva's UI, fonts and assets all have unusually strong Japanese coverage, including a deep Japanese font library. AI narration and auto-captions support Japanese, putting it in the front rank of AI tools for Japanese workflows. Video translation can also adapt English video into Japanese.

What does Canva Pro cost?＋

As of June 2026 Canva Pro runs around $13/mo (with an annual-billing discount); Canva Teams is priced per seat. Premium assets, Magic Studio AI, brand kit and high-res export start at Pro. Always confirm the latest on the official site.

Canva Video AI vs Fliki/InVideo — what's the difference?＋

Fliki and InVideo specialize in 'text → video' and target narration-led explainer production. Canva is 'design platform with video AI bolted on' and fits anyone running social and promo creative across image and video in one tool. It shines on short-form, vertical and promotional video over long narration.

Can I post Canva-made video to social channels?＋

Yes. Canva has rich aspect-ratio templates for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and X (Twitter), and an in-app social scheduler. The social-first workflow is one of its strongest fits.
