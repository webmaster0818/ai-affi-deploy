What is InVideo AI? One prompt, one finished video

InVideo AI is an AI tool that takes a single prompt like "make a 10-minute video about X" or "make a TikTok video about the benefits of sauna" and generates a finished video complete with narration, captions and scenes.

You don't need a script or an existing blog post; speed is the headline. After generation, refine with conversational edits like "swap scene 3", "narration in a calmer voice", "change the caption color". Non-designers can move output upward without ever touching a timeline.

Key features

1. Prompt → finished video

Specify theme, length, target viewer and tone in a single line and the AI handles script writing, scene split, asset selection, narration generation and captioning to produce a finished video. Even the first draft is publishable; polish work is incremental.

2. Chat-style editing

Iterate by typing requests like "swap scene 3 for beach footage", "narration in a female voice", "softer BGM". The AI applies the change. No timeline editing required — a UX no other tool quite matches.

3. YouTube horizontal / TikTok and Shorts vertical, both first-class

Specify "YouTube horizontal" or "TikTok vertical" in the prompt and the AI optimizes structure and template for each. Multi-platform publishing flows much faster.

4. Built-in asset library and voice synthesis

Stock footage, images and music ship in the box, auto-assigned from your prompt. AI narration covers 50+ languages with voice tone options.

Pricing (as of June 2026)

InVideo AI ships Free / Plus / Max tiers, with Plus starting around $20/mo on annual billing.

  • Free: monthly cap of generation minutes, watermarked.
  • Plus: solo-creator default. Watermark removed, ample generation and AI credits.
  • Max: for production and commercial use. Large generation quota plus pro features.

Plans are managed by "monthly generation minutes" and AI credits. Because of the one-prompt-many-videos UX, generation minutes matter more than they do for other text-to-video tools. Pricing and plan composition change — always confirm with the official site before signing up.

Who InVideo AI is for

  • Anyone who can't justify writing a script and just wants the prompt-to-video pipeline
  • Creators producing YouTube horizontal and TikTok / Shorts vertical of the same topic in parallel
  • Anyone curious about chat-style editing for video
  • News round-ups, how-tos, listicles and other formulaic formats that benefit from high turn rate

For granular control over scripts use Fliki; for long-form summarization and clipping use Pictory; for avatar video use Synthesia or HeyGen.

Frequently asked questions

What can I do on InVideo AI's free plan?＋

The free plan gives you a monthly cap of generation minutes for video creation and export. Output carries an InVideo AI watermark and has limits on generation time, AI features and asset access. For real publishing volume, upgrade to a paid plan. Check the official site for the latest limits.

Does InVideo AI support Japanese?＋

InVideo AI covers 50+ languages of AI narration and captions, including Japanese video generation. UI and help are English-first; English prompts generally produce more reliable output than Japanese. Test Japanese narration on the free plan before committing.

What does InVideo AI cost?＋

As of June 2026 the lineup is Free / Plus / Max, with Plus starting around $20/mo on annual billing. Generation minutes, AI credits, watermark removal and asset access scale with the plan. Always confirm the latest on the official site.

InVideo AI vs Fliki — what's the difference?＋

Both are text-to-video, but InVideo AI commits hardest to the 'one prompt → finished video' plus 'chat-style editing' UX. It's the speed-and-volume play. Fliki gives you more granular control over scripts and individual scenes. Pick InVideo for speed, Fliki for craft.

Can I use InVideo AI commercially?＋

On paid plans, commercial use is generally allowed. Individual asset licenses (music, stock footage) and the terms can change, though — always check the official license and terms of service before commercial deployment.
