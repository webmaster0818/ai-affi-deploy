What is Fliki? An AI tool that turns text into video

Fliki is a tool that takes a script or a blog post URL and assembles AI narration plus stock footage into a finished video. The pitch is simple: if you can write, you can ship video. No NLE skills, no recording booth.

AI video tools split into a handful of categories, and Fliki is the flagship of the "text-to-video" group. If you want to turn a blog post into a YouTube video, or churn out narrated explainers at volume, Fliki is the most direct route. We rank it #1 on this site because of how well it balances onboarding ease, range of use cases and recurring affiliate fit.

Key features

1. Auto-generate videos from text or articles

Paste in a script or a blog post URL and Fliki breaks the content into scenes, assigns stock footage and narration to each, and stitches it all into a draft. You can edit any scene's text, visuals or audio afterwards — auto-generation plus manual control in one workflow.

2. 2,000+ realistic AI voices

Fliki ships with 75+ languages and 2,000+ AI voices, Japanese included. Tone and timbre options let you match brand or video style. It's especially strong if you publish the same script in multiple languages.

3. Built-in stock footage and music

A library of video, image and music assets ships in the box, so you don't have to bounce between separate stock sites. Fliki picks footage that matches your keywords automatically, cutting hours off asset gathering.

4. Voice cloning and podcast generation

Beyond video, Fliki can generate spoken-word content (podcasts and the like) from your text. Useful if you also publish audio.

Pricing (as of June 2026)

Fliki has a free Free plan, a Standard plan (around $19.99/mo) and a Premium plan (around $66/mo). Higher tiers unlock more monthly credits and premium voice or feature access. Annual billing knocks the monthly equivalent down a notch.

  • Free: monthly credit allowance to test the product. Output is watermarked and otherwise limited.
  • Standard (~$19.99/mo): the default for solo creators. Watermark removed, standard generation quota.
  • Premium (~$66/mo): large generation quota, premium voices and pro features. Built for production and commercial use.

Generation across all plans is managed via credits, so heavy or long-form production naturally pushes you to higher tiers. Pricing and plan composition change without notice — always confirm the current plans on the official site before signing up.

Who Fliki is for

  • Bloggers who want to repurpose articles into YouTube or short-form video
  • Creators producing narration-led explainers or round-up videos at volume
  • Beginners without any shooting or editing experience
  • Anyone publishing the same content across multiple languages

On the other hand, if your videos lean on live-action vlogs, cinematic visuals or elaborate motion graphics, you'll want to pair Fliki with Runway or a dedicated NLE.

Affiliate program and the recurring-commission edge

Fliki publishes an affiliate program with a Lifetime 30% recurring commission. As long as the person you referred keeps their subscription active, you keep earning.

Recurring commissions compound, which is why they're attractive for long-running content sites. Fliki is also approachable enough to recommend to beginners, making it an unusually well-fitted "hero product" for AI-video review media. That said, commission rates and program terms can change, so always confirm the official affiliate agreement before relying on them.

A typical workflow

Our complete Fliki tutorial walks the full flow, but the gist:

  • ① Create an account on the official site (start free)
  • ② Pick a mode like "Text to Video" and paste in a script or article URL
  • ③ Fliki splits the content into scenes, assigns assets and renders narration

The verdict

Fliki nails the one thing it sets out to do — turning text into video — and does it in a way beginners can follow on day one. Deep multilingual narration, an honest free plan and a recurring-commission affiliate program combine into our pick for the "first AI video tool you should try". It won't generate cinematic footage, but for explainers, round-ups and product overviews that you actually want to ship, it's the strongest starting point in the category.

Frequently asked questions

What can I actually do on Fliki's free plan?＋

You get a monthly credit allowance for generating videos and audio, with a Fliki watermark on output and limits on length and downloads. It's the right way to test voice quality and the editor before paying. Specifics change over time, so confirm the latest on the official site.

Does Fliki handle Japanese narration?＋

Yes. Fliki supports 75+ languages, Japanese included, with multiple voice options. You can pick a tone that suits explainer videos or product demos. As always with TTS, pronunciation feel varies between voices — the free plan is the cheapest way to test before committing.

What does pricing look like?＋

As of June 2026 there are three tiers: Free, Standard (around $19.99/mo) and Premium (around $66/mo). Higher tiers unlock more generation credits, premium voices and advanced features. Annual billing comes with a discount. Always confirm the current plans on the official site before signing up.

How much does the Fliki affiliate program pay?＋

Fliki publishes a recurring commission of 30% for the lifetime of each referred subscription. Recurring affiliate programs compound nicely if you run a content site in the AI video category. Commission rates and payout conditions can change — always confirm the latest in the official affiliate terms.

Which kinds of YouTube videos suit Fliki?＋

Script-driven explainers, news round-ups, listicles and other narration-led formats. If you already publish a blog, Fliki's article-to-video flow lets you cross-publish to YouTube with minimal extra work. Live-action vlogs or cinematic productions are not its sweet spot — you'll want different tools for those.
