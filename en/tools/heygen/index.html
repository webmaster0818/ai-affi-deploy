What is HeyGen? Avatars meet translation

HeyGen is an AI video tool that combines realistic AI avatar generation with video translation plus lip-sync. You can render scripts on camera with an avatar, or take existing footage and adapt it into another language while matching mouth movement — exactly what you want when shipping content to international audiences.

Key features

1. Premium AI avatars

Pick from a diverse library of AI avatars to deliver your script. Expressiveness keeps improving release after release, and there's a custom feature to train an avatar on your voice and likeness.

2. Video translation with lip-sync

Upload existing video, choose a target language, and HeyGen matches the speaker's mouth movement to the translated audio. You ship video where it looks like the speaker is natively delivering in the new language — a category-leading capability.

3. Multilingual coverage

Both avatar narration and translation support many languages, making HeyGen a fit for any content workflow with a global rollout in mind.

Frequently asked questions

What is HeyGen's video translation lip-sync?＋

It's a feature that takes existing video, translates it into another language, and matches the speaker's mouth movement to the translated audio. Instead of layering captions or dubs on top, the speaker looks like they're natively delivering in the new language. It's a substantial upgrade for international localization.

HeyGen vs Synthesia — what's the difference?＋

Both deliver AI avatar video, but HeyGen leans harder into video translation, lip-sync and expressive avatars, while Synthesia is the default for training, SOPs and enterprise governance. Pick HeyGen for localization and creator-grade output, Synthesia for organizational deployment.

What does HeyGen cost?＋

As of June 2026 HeyGen offers Free, Creator (around $24/mo), Team and Enterprise tiers. Generation is credit-based, with plan-dependent caps on minutes, resolution and avatar/feature access. Always check the latest on the official site.

Does HeyGen get expensive?＋

Credit-based pricing means heavy production volume can rack up cost fast. Use a free or lower tier first to validate quality and burn rate, then choose a plan that matches your monthly minutes. High-resolution and long-form output usually assumes a higher tier.
