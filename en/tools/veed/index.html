What is Veed.io? An all-in-one browser editor

Veed.io is an online editor that handles video editing entirely in the browser. Cutting, captions, auto-subtitles, screen recording and AI features all live in one tab. From social-first short-form to business explainers, it covers a wide range of use cases.

Key features

1. Browser-based video editing

Trim, cut, add overlays and music in the browser — no install. Works across operating systems and makes team collaboration easy.

2. Auto-captions and translated subtitles

Generate captions from audio and produce translated subtitles in many languages. Especially well-suited to vertical short-form where most viewers watch without sound.

3. Screen recording and AI features

Built-in screen recording lets you handle tutorial capture and editing in one place. AI features cover things like noise removal and background removal.

Pricing (as of June 2026)

Veed.io offers Free / Lite / Pro / Business tiers (Lite around $18/mo). Free ships with watermarks and export-time limits. Higher tiers expand export time, storage and AI feature access. Pricing varies with region and updates — always confirm on the official site before signing up.

Who Veed.io is for

  • Anyone who wants browser-based, low-friction video editing
  • Creators producing captioned short-form for social at volume
  • Anyone unifying screen recording and editing in one tool

Caveats

Pure text-to-video automation isn't as strong as on Fliki or Pictory, and avatar generation trails dedicated tools like Synthesia or HeyGen. Position Veed.io as the "editing and finishing" layer, not the "generation" layer.

The verdict

Veed.io is a flexible editor that gets editing, captions and screen recording done from a single browser tab. Low learning curve and good for finishing social video. Pair it with AI generation tools — generate elsewhere, finish in Veed.io — and the workflow really clicks.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install Veed.io?＋

No. Veed.io runs in the browser — no native install required. As long as you have a modern browser you can cut video, add captions and record your screen in one place.

Is Veed.io's auto-captioning any good?＋

Veed.io is strong on auto-captions, with multilingual support and translated subtitles. Great for sound-off social viewing. Proper nouns can mistranscribe, so plan a final pass before export.

What does Veed.io cost?＋

As of June 2026 the lineup is Free / Lite / Pro / Business (Lite around $18/mo). The free plan ships with watermarks and export limits. Pricing varies by region and updates — confirm on the official site.

Can Veed.io auto-generate video from text?＋

Veed.io includes AI features, but for one-shot text-to-video with narration Fliki or Pictory are stronger. Veed.io's edge is being a flexible editor — pair it with a generative tool for end-to-end production.
