What is Runway? A generative video tool

Runway is a generative video tool that creates footage itself from text or images. Where other tools assemble narration video or render avatar video, Runway synthesizes the visuals from nothing — exactly what you want for scenes that would be impractical to film live.

Key features

1. Text and image to video

Provide a prompt or an image and Runway returns a short clip based on it. Particularly strong for scenes that would be hard to recreate physically — abstract, surreal or otherwise visually distinctive material.

2. Creative and ad asset generation

Ads, music videos, concept films — anything where original visuals are at a premium. Useful for rapid visual prototyping too.

3. Editing and tool interoperability

Generated clips slot in as raw material for other tools like Veed — "generate in Runway, edit and caption elsewhere" is a viable workflow.

Pricing (as of June 2026)

Runway uses Free / Standard (around $15/mo) / Pro / Unlimited credit-based plans. Generation volume drives cost; long-form or mass generation gets expensive. Free plan can include a watermark. Pricing and plan composition change — always confirm with the official site before signing up.

Who Runway is for

  • Creators producing visuals impossible to film live
  • Anyone building ad, music video or concept film assets
  • Anyone prototyping a visual idea quickly

Caveats

Not the right tool for narration-led explainer volume or script-read production — use Fliki for that. Credits burn fast on long-form, and dialing in your intended look requires prompt skill.

The verdict

Runway brings creativity no other category offers — generating footage itself. It's less about communicating information and more about generating visuals. Split tools by job: Fliki for explainers, Synthesia/HeyGen for avatars, Runway for footage generation.

Frequently asked questions

What does Runway actually do?＋

It generates short video clips from text prompts or input images. The strength is creating visuals that would be hard or impossible to film — abstract scenes, surreal imagery, concept work. Video editing tools are bundled too.

Runway vs Fliki — what's the difference?＋

Fliki ships narration-led explainer videos at volume. Runway generates the footage itself for creative, original visual work. One is for delivering information, the other for inventing visuals — completely different intents.

What does Runway cost?＋

As of June 2026 Runway runs Free / Standard (around $15/mo) / Pro / Unlimited credit plans. Heavier generation pushes you toward higher tiers. Pricing changes — always confirm with the official site.

Is Runway beginner-friendly?＋

It's approachable to start, but getting the exact output you want requires prompt skill. Use a free or lower tier to learn how prompt phrasing affects results before committing to a heavier plan.
