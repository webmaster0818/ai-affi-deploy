Complete Runway Tutorial: Generate AI Video

Runway generates short video clips from text or images. This guide covers the basics: sign-up, text/image generation, clip tweaks and export, plus how to try it with free credits.

What Runway is

Runway generates AI video from text prompts or images. It's great for imagery you can't easily film and short stylized clips for social. To mass-produce narrated videos from scripts, use Fliki or Pictory; for avatar videos, Synthesia/HeyGen. Pick by use case.

How to use Runway in 5 steps

Step 1: Create an account

Sign up on the official site. If free credits are available, validate quality for free first; free tiers usually limit generations/resolution or add a watermark. See best free AI video tools.

Step 2: Choose the generation mode (text/image)

Pick a mode for your goal: Text to Video to create from scratch, or Image to Video to animate an existing image.

Step 3: Write the prompt

Describe the shot specifically in your prompt. To get closer to your vision:

  • Specify subject, motion, camera work and mood
  • Keep one clip to one scene (don't overload)
  • If it misses, reword and regenerate

Step 4: Generate and refine

Review the clip and regenerate or tweak as needed. AI generation rarely nails it first try, so adjust the prompt gradually toward the result you want.

Step 5: Export and stitch

Export the clip. To combine several clips into one, use Runway's editing features or an editor like Veed.io or Descript. Free tiers may add a watermark or resolution limits — check paid-plan terms if that matters.

Tips for better generations

  • Be specific in prompts: vague prompts drift
  • Make short, combine: more stable than one long generation
  • Use a reference image: Image to Video locks the direction
  • Expect iteration: try a few and keep the best

FAQ

Can I use Runway for free?＋

Runway often offers free credits to try it. Free tiers usually limit generations, resolution or add a watermark, so validate quality and workflow for free, then upgrade for serious use.

Can I make video from text alone?＋

Runway supports text-to-video and image-to-video. It's best for short clips; to get close to your vision, write specific prompts and iterate between generation and tweaks.

What is Runway best for?＋

It excels at imagery that's hard to film and short stylized clips for social. To mass-produce narrated explainers from scripts, use Fliki or Pictory; for avatar videos, Synthesia or HeyGen.
