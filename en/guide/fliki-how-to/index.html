Complete Fliki tutorial: from signup to your first video

Fliki is one of the easiest AI video tools to learn. This tutorial walks through account signup, text-to-video creation, narration setup and export, calling out the points beginners tend to trip over.

What Fliki does (quick recap)

Fliki takes text or a blog post URL and produces a video with AI narration and captions. It covers 75+ languages and 2,000+ AI voices and works well in many languages. For pricing and features, see our full Fliki review. This guide focuses on the actual workflow.

Step 1: Create an account

Head to the Fliki website and sign up. Start free — you can confirm the editor and voice quality before paying. Sign up with an email address or supported provider, then jump into the dashboard.

Step 2: Pick a creation mode

Fliki ships a few creation modes; the two most common are:

  • Text to Video: paste a script or article URL and produce a video.
  • Text to Speech: generate narration audio only.

For YouTube and social video, pick "Text to Video". Paste in an article URL and Fliki drafts the video for you.

Step 3: Enter the script and review scenes

With the script in place, Fliki slices it into scenes and assigns visuals and captions. From the draft you can adjust scene by scene:

  • The narration text for each scene
  • The assigned footage or image (swap in something better)
  • Adding, deleting and reordering scenes

When auto-assigned assets don't fit your message, swap in alternatives from the built-in library to push the polish up.

Step 4: Pick narration language and voice

Choose your narration language and pick a voice. Fliki ships multiple voices per language, so you can match the tone of an explainer or a product demo to the right voice. Some voices let you tune reading speed and intonation. Proper nouns and English words inside a non-English script can mispronounce — always play back narration before export.

Step 5: Music and captions

Pick BGM from the built-in library and balance the audio. Turn captions on for sound-off viewing. Tune font and position so they read cleanly.

Step 6: Preview and export

Preview the full video and verify the timing of narration, captions and visuals. When you're happy, export. The free plan watermarks output and may cap exports — upgrade to a paid plan for production use. Exported video uploads straight to YouTube or social.

Production tips

  • Use your blog: feed article URLs to skip script writing.
  • Lock your voice: pick a voice per channel to keep brand consistency.
  • Watch credits: generation is credit-based — track burn rate for long-form / volume.
  • Go multilingual: export the same script in another language to grow reach overseas.

Try Fliki's narration quality on the free plan first.

FAQ

Can I start Fliki for free?＋

Yes. Signing up gives you free-plan access; you can test the editor and voice quality immediately. Monthly credit caps and watermarks are typical on the free plan. Upgrade for production use.

What if narration mispronounces something?＋

Proper nouns, foreign words and numbers can read incorrectly. Try changing the spelling to phonetic form, rewriting the phrase or trying a different voice. Always play back before export.

Can I use the exported video on YouTube?＋

Generally yes — exported video uploads to YouTube and other platforms, subject to commercial-use terms in the plan and asset licensing. Confirm the official terms of service before publishing.

What does Fliki cost?＋

As of June 2026 the lineup is Free / Standard (around $19.99/mo) / Premium (around $66/mo). Pricing changes — always confirm with the official site. The Fliki review covers it in more depth.
