Complete HeyGen Tutorial: Make an AI Avatar Video

HeyGen lets you create a video of an AI avatar speaking your script — no filming required. This guide walks through the basics in five steps: sign-up, script, avatar and voice selection, and export, plus narration tips and how to try it free.

What HeyGen is

HeyGen generates a video where an AI avatar speaks your script. With no filming or on-camera talent, you can make narrated explainers and presentation videos, which is why it's used for product intros, training and multilingual content. For how it compares with Synthesia, see Synthesia vs HeyGen.

How to use HeyGen in 5 steps

Step 1: Create an account

Sign up on the official site. If a free trial or demo is available, validate the workflow for free first. Free tiers typically add limits like export caps or watermarks. For the broader picture, see best free AI video tools.

Step 2: Pick a template and avatar

Choose a template that fits your goal and select the AI avatar to feature. Match the avatar's look, outfit and background to your use case (explainer, presentation, social). Starting from a template close to your sample keeps it simple.

Step 3: Enter your script

Type the script for the avatar to speak. AI narration timing changes with punctuation and line breaks, so:

  • Keep sentences short (long ones sound flat)
  • Use punctuation to create natural pauses
  • Check the pronunciation of names and numbers

Step 4: Choose language and voice

Select the language and voice for narration. Naturalness varies by voice and plan, so preview before exporting and tweak the script where intonation sounds off.

Step 5: Preview and export

Review the full preview and, if it looks good, export. Free tiers may add watermarks or resolution limits, so check the paid-plan terms if that matters for your publish.

Tips for a better result

  • Keep the script readable aloud: shorten anything you stumble over
  • One message per video: focus on the key point
  • Add captions: works with sound off (great for social)
  • Make two versions: compare avatars and voices to find the best fit

Notes on commercial use

For monetization, ads or product intros, check your plan's commercial-use terms and use the provided avatars and voices within them. See our AI video commercial-use guide.
