Complete Descript Tutorial: Edit Video by Editing Text

Descript lets you edit video and audio as if you were editing a transcript. This guide covers the basics — creating a project, transcription, text-based editing, filler removal, captions and export.

What Descript is

Descript's signature is text-based editing: edit the transcript and the video/audio edits with it. Delete a sentence and the matching footage is cut too, so you edit intuitively without micromanaging a timeline. It's strong for spoken content — podcasts, explainers and interviews. To generate video from text, see Fliki or Pictory; for browser editing, Veed.io.

How to use Descript in 5 steps

Step 1: Create a project

Sign up and open a new project. If a free plan is available, validate the workflow for free first; free tiers usually cap transcription time or export. See best free AI video tools.

Step 2: Import media and transcribe

Upload the video or audio you want to edit and Descript transcribes it automatically, with multilingual support. Auto-transcription can contain errors, so skim the text first.

Step 3: Edit the text to cut

The core feature is text-based editing: delete a sentence in the transcript and the matching video/audio is cut too. Use it like this:

  • Delete misspeaks and tangents by removing the sentences
  • Move text to reorder sections
  • Keep only the key points by tightening the script

Step 4: Remove fillers and tidy captions

Use the filler-word removal ("um", "uh") and silence-trimming features to improve pace. Generate captions and fix errors so the video works with sound off. See auto subtitles & captions.

Step 5: Preview and export

Preview the full project and, if it looks good, export. Free tiers may add a watermark or resolution limits, so check paid-plan terms if that matters.

Tips for a better result

  • Compose with text: slim the cut by tidying the script
  • Remove fillers: cut connective words for pace
  • Use captions: understandable with sound off
  • Final check: fix transcription errors before export

Notes on commercial use

For monetization or ads, check your plan's commercial terms and the licenses of music, assets, fonts and any voice synthesis you use. See our AI video commercial-use guide.
