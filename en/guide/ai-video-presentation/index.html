How to Make Presentation Videos with AI

Presentation videos for meetings, training and product explainers can be made efficiently from slides or scripts with AI. This guide covers how to choose tools for narrated or avatar-led presentation videos, and the steps to make one — based on public information.

Two main approaches

The right tool depends on your goal:

  • Slides + narration: import slides/script and add AI voiceover. Best for turning decks into videos.
  • Avatar-led: an AI avatar narrates beside your slides — adds a human presence for training and explainers.

Decide first whether you want an on-screen presenter (avatar); that narrows tool choice.

Recommended tools by goal

Slides + narration

For narrated video from text/scripts, Fliki and Pictory fit well — they auto-generate AI voiceover and captions. See the Fliki tutorial and Pictory tutorial.

Avatar-led presentations

To have an AI avatar narrate beside your slides, Synthesia and HeyGen are the go-tos for training, internal briefings and multilingual decks. See the HeyGen tutorial.

For detailed editing

To craft charts and captions in detail, editor-first tools like Veed.io and Canva Video help you build polished presentation videos from templates.

Steps to make a presentation video

  1. Outline and script: one message per slide; keep the script short.
  2. Import slides/script into the tool (text input or slide import).
  3. Choose narration (language and voice) to generate the voiceover.
  4. Adjust avatar, captions and visuals as needed.
  5. Preview and export; fix awkward pacing by editing the script.

Tips for an effective presentation video

  • One message per slide: don't overload
  • Add captions: understandable with sound off
  • Preview narration: check names and numbers
  • Favor brevity: concise beats long-winded

Notes on external/commercial use

For external or ad use, check your plan's commercial terms and the licenses of images, music and avatars you use. See our AI video commercial-use guide.

Summary

For presentation videos: Fliki/Pictory for narrated, Synthesia/HeyGen for avatar-led. Validate narration and workflow on the free tier first. Compare tools in the comparison ranking.

FAQ

Can I turn PowerPoint slides into a video with AI?＋

Some tools can import slides or text and convert them into a narrated video with AI voice and captions. Fine-tune pacing and emphasis by editing the script.

Do I have to record narration myself?＋

No — AI narration generates the voiceover from your script. Pick a language and voice; many tools support multiple languages. Naturalness varies by tool and plan, so preview before subscribing.

Can I add a presenter (avatar) to the presentation?＋

AI avatar tools like Synthesia and HeyGen let an avatar narrate beside your slides, useful for training and explainers where you want a human presence.

Can I make one for free?＋

Many tools offer a free plan or trial, usually with export caps or a watermark. Validate quality for free, then upgrade when you publish.
