What free plans can and can't do

Most AI video generators offer a free plan or trial. Even for free, you can usually test the core features — generating video from text, editing with templates, trying AI narration. But free plans typically come with limits:

  • Watermark: a logo is added to exported video
  • Export / minutes cap: a monthly limit on output
  • Resolution limits: full HD/4K is paid-only
  • Commercial-use limits: monetization/ads may require a paid plan

In other words, treat the free plan as a way to check quality and workflow. Upgrade when you publish or monetize for real — that's the most efficient path.

Tools you can try for free

Among the tools we benchmark, here are the main ones you can try on a free plan or trial, grouped by goal. Confirm the latest free-tier terms on each tool's review and official site.

  • In-browser editing: Veed.io (free plan; auto subtitles) / Canva Video (free plan)
  • Generative video: Runway (free credits)
  • AI avatars: Synthesia / HeyGen (demo/trial)

How to make the most of the free tier

  1. Pick one goal first: decide your first video (blog-to-video, Shorts, etc.) before testing — it's easier to find the right fit.
  2. Try two tools side by side: while free, compare narration quality, ease of editing and export quality.
  3. Check the watermark: make sure it's acceptable for your channels before going live.
  4. Verify commercial rights: if you'll monetize, confirm the license terms on both free and paid plans.

When to upgrade to paid

Consider upgrading when any of these apply:

  • You want to remove the watermark for a clean publish
  • You've hit the free export/minutes cap
  • You need full HD/4K output
  • You want commercial use (monetization, ads)

If you'll keep using it, annual billing usually lowers the effective monthly cost — see "choosing by price" in our ranking. For step-by-step workflows, see How to make YouTube videos with AI and the Fliki how-to guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can you really use AI video generators for free?＋

Many tools offer a free plan or free trial. Expect limits, though: export caps, watermarks and resolution restrictions are common. Use the free tier to check quality and workflow, then upgrade when you publish for real.

Do free plans add a watermark?＋

It depends on the tool and plan. Free tiers often add a watermark or restrict commercial use, so always check the terms if you plan to monetize or run ads.

Can I make narration in my language for free?＋

Multilingual tools like Fliki and Veed often let you try narration on the free tier. Naturalness varies, so preview the voice before committing.

When should I upgrade from free to paid?＋

Upgrade when you want to remove the watermark, you've hit the export cap, you need full HD/4K, or you want commercial rights. If you'll keep using it, annual billing usually lowers the effective monthly cost.
