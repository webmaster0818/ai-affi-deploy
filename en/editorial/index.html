About the editorial team

The MoviAI editorial team researches and compares AI video generation tools across every category — text-to-video, AI avatar, in-browser editors and generative video models. We continuously track official pricing updates and new tool releases, and we never take content direction from the tool vendors we cover.

Our 5 rating criteria

Every review and ranking is scored across these five axes. The star rating (out of 5) is the editorial team's composite score based on them.

  • Price & value: free plan availability, monthly/annual cost, cost per unit of output.
  • Features & output quality: supported video types, render quality, editing flexibility.
  • Language support: narration, subtitles and UI languages, and how natural they sound.
  • Ease of use: learning curve, templates, whether it runs fully in the browser.
  • Fit for purpose: suitability for YouTube, Shorts, training, product demos and more.

Sourcing & verification

  • Primary sources first: pricing, features, language support and affiliate terms are taken from each tool's official website.
  • Dated information: content is marked as accurate as of June 2026, and we prompt readers to confirm the latest details.
  • Regular review: we re-check price changes, new features and plan changes, and update pages when they change.
  • No hype: we avoid absolute claims and never publish fabricated reviews or testimonials.

Advertising disclosure

This site is funded by affiliate revenue, and some links are ads. However, compensation does not unfairly influence our ratings or rankings — scoring follows the public-information criteria above. Ad links are disclosed on every page. See our About page for details.

Corrections

If you spot an error or an official change we've missed, please reach us via the contact details on our About page. We verify and update promptly.
